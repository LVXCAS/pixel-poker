Verdict

Strongest table: B (Smoke & Felt). It is the only one where the light, the felt, the slip and the coach slate read as one lit room, and its win frame (lifted cards, chips sliding in, chalk "+360") has the most physical payoff. Strongest identity and title: C. If you want something people screenshot and remember, C's matchbook coach and marquee sign do that best.

  • A, One Bulb: the most disciplined of the three, but also the emptiest. The "chalk slate" coach still reads as a navy dashboard box, the table has no material detail beyond the slip, and the giant "flush" floats in the bottom-left corner, far from the cards that made it.
  • B, Smoke & Felt: the smoke reads as brown smears or stains on the felt, not haze, especially on the title. The demo coach says "call" at 26% equity against a 33% price, which is a bad lesson for a trainer. The win PNG was also captured mid count-up, so it shows a stale "pot 100" and a stack of 1,380 instead of 1,480.
  • C, Arcade card room: too loud. Every seat, button and panel has its own framed plate on patterned wallpaper, so nothing leads the eye and the one-bulb mood is gone. The odds ticket's scale labels are nearly invisible, the "23%" price label runs onto the rail, and the "25%" on the title is clipped at the right edge.
  • All three: the avatars for crt carl and pixel pete are cut off at the left edge of the screen.
